> Most Sundays, 10 am - 2 pm
> (note: ReBikeSD is closed on the last Sunday of the month)
>
> Schedule an Appointment [link to /repair#reservations]
>
> On most Sundays, ReBikeSD meets at the Bike Coalition office [link to /contact] for a free, hands-on bike clinic.
>
> Need to fix your bike? We'll help you troubleshoot and guide you through the repairs.
>
> Want to learn? You'll get hands-on experience and leave with skills to keep your bike rolling.
>
> Looking for new wheels? ReBikeSD offers a selection of refurbished bikes you can take home at an affordable price.
>
> Space is limited! Make a reservation to guarantee your spot!
>
> Notice
>
> ReBikeSD offers free repair assistance to the community. On any given Sunday the expertise of our volunteer mechanics will vary. As a result, during any given repair session ReBike reserves the right to say we will not be able to work on any given bike based on our available mechanics experience and/or physical ability.
>
> E-Bike Policy: ReBikeSD does NOT offer assistance with e-bikes or e-scooters.
>
> Each member of our all-volunteer staff can agree or decline to work or not work on any e-bike or bicycles. Please respect our decision in these situations.
>
> ReBikeSD [link to http://rebikesd.org/] is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to rescuing and redistributing bikes to those who need them most.
