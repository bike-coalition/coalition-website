# Redesign review: future page drafts

October 21, 2026. A hard look at the 38 future drafts on the design canvas, from the point of view of a nonprofit fundraiser, a UX lead and a storyteller. Graded from the page source; the drafts were not viewed rendered. No comment threads had been left on the canvas when this was written.

## Overall grade: C+

The drafts are tidy, on-brand and honest about what they don't know. They are not yet a website anyone would explore for fun, and they don't make the case for giving.

| Area | Grade | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual system | B | Teal and Spring Green, wide/narrow headlines and the sprocket arch look like the Coalition. Used identically on all 38 pages, the arch stops being a signature and becomes wallpaper. |
| Storytelling | D | No page tells a story. The homepage is a brochure: vision, goals, programs, merch. It never names the problem the Coalition exists to fight, so there is nothing to root for. |
| Journey and curiosity | D | Almost every page ends at the footer. Nothing pulls a visitor to the next page, so a reader who finishes Safety Tips has no reason to see Classes, and a reader who finishes After a Crash is never invited to act. |
| Fundraising | C- | Donate opens well, then dilutes itself with a produce-box coupon and bike donations at the same weight as giving. No "what your gift does", no Giving Tuesday, no trust signals near the form. Donors and Donate compete. |
| Headlines and copy | C | Most H1s are page labels with a period ("990 forms.", "Contact & FAQ.", "Bike classes."). Labels belong in the eyebrow; the headline should be a promise. |
| Accessibility | C | Stacked headline lines are separate spans with no space between them, so screen readers read "Bikeclasses" and "Annualreports". One H1 per page and alt text are good. |
| Page weight and hierarchy | C | Utility pages (990 forms, bylaws, receipt form, valet check-in, taco orders) get the same giant hero as the homepage. A donor looking for a 990 has to scroll past a billboard. |
| Honesty and accuracy | B+ | `[FILL IN]` markers instead of invented facts. Keep it that way. |

## What to fix, in order

1. **Give every page a next step.** Add a shared "Keep rolling" band before the footer with three hand-picked next pages, written as invitations, not links. Learning pages lead to riding, riding to joining, reading to acting.
2. **Rebuild the homepage as a story.** Joy, then the problem (traffic violence, with an approved statistic), then the fix (the five goals), then what's happening now, then the visitor's part, then the ask. Merch moves out of the closing spot; the page should end on the newsletter and a gift.
3. **Make Donate convert.** Giving Tuesday band at the top, an impact ladder (amount to outcome, filled in by the Coalition), trust signals by the form (501(c)(3), EIN, Candid Platinum), and the coupon, bike donations and stock gifts folded into one compact "More ways to give" grid.
4. **Turn labels into promises.** Keep the page name as the eyebrow and write the H1 as what the visitor gets.
5. **Give utility pages a compact header.** No arch, no billboard: a short teal band with the title and one line, then the content.
6. **Fix the headline spacing bug** on every page.

## Done on the canvas (October 21)

- **Keep rolling band** (Future: Keep rolling band, shared) on all 37 inner pages: three curated next pages per page, with a lead line matched to the page type (learn, act, give, about).
- **Homepage rebuilt as a story:** "Every street is a choice." hero, then 01 The joy, 02 The problem (with an `[FILL IN]` stat slot), 03 The fix (the five goals), Happening now (calendar summary block), 04 Your part, a rider quote, and 05 Keep it rolling with a Giving Tuesday chip and trust line. Merch left the homepage; it stays in the footer.
- **Donate:** Giving Tuesday band, impact ladder with `[FILL IN]` tiles, trust strip by the form, one primary path, everything else in a compact "More ways to give" grid. 7,000 px down to 5,800 before the next-step band.
- **Donors** is now the major-gifts and fund-choice page; **Membership** leads with belonging and puts the levels first.
- **Compact headers** on 8 utility pages. The invented taco pickup line is gone.
- **Promise headlines** on 20 pages (for example Classes: "Ride your city.", Contact: "Ask us anything.", Staff & board: "Your street team.").
- **Headline spacing** fixed on every page.

## New things to confirm

- "Members keep the Coalition independent" is draft copy, not a fact from the live site. Keep it only if the Coalition agrees.
- The homepage hero now uses "Every street is a choice." (hero option A on the canvas), with "Bike for a better San Diego" in the eyebrow.
- Giving Tuesday goal or match, and the impact ladder's amounts and outcomes.
- The Champion membership tag "Go all in" is new wording.

## Regrade, second pass (October 21)

After the first round of fixes the drafts graded **B-**: the journey and fundraising problems were solved, but 30 inner pages still opened with the same teal hero and arch, the homepage had no Bike the Bay and an empty "happening now", Advocacy had no sense of urgency, and the longest pages were walls.

After the second pass: **B+.**

| Area | First | Now | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visual system | B | A- | Hero families by section: Learn pages open on Spring Green with a wide photo, Act pages on a full-bleed photo under Deep Teal, About and Join pages keep the sprocket arch, which is now a signature again. Utility pages stay compact. |
| Storytelling | D | B+ | Homepage arc, plus Bike the Bay ("Once a year, the bridge is ours.") and the 18-cities ticker as the one moment of motion (still when reduced motion is on). |
| Journey and curiosity | D | A- | "I want to: Ride, Learn, Speak up, Give" right under the homepage hero; Keep rolling band on every page; "On this page" jump links on the seven longest pages. |
| Fundraising | C- | B+ | Unchanged this pass; held back by blank impact amounts. |
| Urgency | n/a | B+ | Advocacy now opens with "Show up where it's decided": real items from Agenda Watch, sampled October 21, 2026. |
| Proof and people | D | C+ | Real upcoming events on the homepage; named staff contacts on Classes, Teach, Advocacy and Bike Share. Still one testimonial and no approved stats. |
| Accessibility | C | B+ | Spacing fixed; photo overlays raised to 86% so Spring Green headlines clear 4.5:1 even over white. Not yet tested at phone width or with a screen reader. |

### What keeps it from an A

1. Real stories: collect three to five rider stories with photos (a first-time commuter, a parent on a school route, a crash survivor who now advocates).
2. Approved numbers for the homepage stat, the impact ladder and corporate claims.
3. A Bike the Bay photo and date; a merch photo.
4. Agenda Watch as a live feed rather than a dated sample, with real "attend or comment" links.
5. A rendered check at desktop and phone width, and a mobile header with a menu button.

## Not fixed yet

- Testimonials and rider stories: the site has eight testimonials, but only one quote's text was captured. Collect real stories and photos; don't write them.
- Impact numbers: every stat slot needs an approved figure.
- Mobile: none of this has been checked at phone width in a browser.
