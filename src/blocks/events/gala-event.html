An evening for safer streets

Join the San Diego County Bike Coalition for the second annual Golden Gear Gala, celebrating the people and partners who make San Diego County a better place to ride.

We’ll honor the 2026 Golden Gear winners at Négociant Winery in Hillcrest. Every ticket supports the Bike Coalition’s advocacy, education and community events across all 18 cities in the county.
