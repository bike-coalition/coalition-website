CicloSDias is San Diego's open streets celebration, inspired by Ciclovía events around the world. Since 2013, it has turned miles of city streets into places where people of all ages and abilities can walk, ride, and play.

Over one mile of San Ysidro Boulevard. No cars. All day.

Sunday, October 18, 2026 · 10 am to 3 pm

Come as you are.

Bring the kids, the grandparents, the scooter, the old cruiser. Drop in anywhere along the route between 10 am and 3 pm and experience San Ysidro like few people ever do: free from cars and full of life.

The community tour and punch card challenge.

Get your card punched at each of the four Points of Interest to receive a custom CicloSDias promotional item!

1

La Semilla Climate Resiliency Center

213 W. Park Ave., San Diego, CA 92173

A Casa Familiar project and innovation incubator for environmental justice in the heart of San Ysidro. La Semilla means “the seed.”

2

San Ysidro Civic Center

212 W. Park Ave., San Diego, CA 92173

Central to grassroots community organizing, and home to free health and fitness classes led by Promotoras.

3

Mr. B Baby Mural

2435 E. Beyer Blvd., San Diego, CA 92173

A tribute to the intertwined cultures of San Ysidro and Tijuana by muralist Michelle Guerrero.

4

Border to Bayshore Complete Streets

San Ysidro

An approximately 6.7-mile bikeway project linking the Bayshore Bikeway, San Ysidro, and the San Ysidro Port of Entry.
