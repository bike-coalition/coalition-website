How it works

1

Get your details ready

You will need your organization's name and address, a contact person, and your preferred month, time of day, class, and format. It takes a few minutes.

2

Send your request

Use the button for your path above. Our partners at SANDAG take the request. Classes are first come, first served, so earlier is better.

3

We schedule it with you

A SANDAG representative follows up within three business days to lock in a date. Then we show up and teach.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. These classes are free through the Coalition's partnership with SANDAG.

How soon will I hear back?

A SANDAG representative follows up within three business days to schedule your class.

What if we need a different class or time?

Email info@sdbikecoalition.org and we will point you in the right direction.
